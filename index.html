What's inside

Two products, really. One for you, one for your guest.

A manager's workspace where content is written once, and a guest guidebook that opens instantly on any phone — no app required.

FOR THE MANAGER
One editor for every property
Set up check-in, WiFi, house rules and recommendations once per property, in a simple editor.
Always current
Update a detail — a new door code, a closed restaurant — and guests see the change immediately. No reprinting, no outdated PDFs.
Shared recommendation library
Your local picks live in one place and attach to any unit nearby, with walking times measured from that unit's front door.
One link or QR per unit

The questions managers actually ask

How is pricing calculated?
Your first guidebook is free, forever. Every guidebook after that is billed per month, and there's no limit on how many you publish.
What's the difference between monthly and yearly?
For each guidebook after your free one: yearly is €5.99 per month, paid upfront for the year. Monthly is €7.99, billed every month.
Can I cancel anytime?
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans run for the year you've paid for.
Why is it called GetLost?
Because the good part of travel is wandering. The bad part is not knowing how the boiler works. We handle the second one.
